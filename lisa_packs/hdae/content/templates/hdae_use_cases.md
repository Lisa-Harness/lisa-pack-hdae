# HDAE Use Cases

Clarify real user workflows, outcomes, failure behavior and meaningful boundaries
in the existing product and architecture. Explore concrete positive, negative
and recovery scenarios, including relevant compatibility, privacy and
operational concerns. Ask about ambiguities that would change scope or success.

Keep workflow knowledge in its existing authoritative home or link it from
project intent; a separate use-case document is optional. Relate scenarios to
planned criteria and evidence, and identify uncovered or intentionally excluded
surfaces. Publish confirmed updates through `hdae_publish_artifact`, reconcile
live progress with `hdae_status`, and use `hdae_export` for review. Coverage
quality is human/model judgment, not a guarantee of schema validation.

Use Establish to discover workflows, Maintain to interpret observed defects,
and Retrospective to improve them from evidence. Consult Minimal artifact model,
Traceability and completeness for long projects, and Gate quality in the pinned
reference. Resume from live project records rather than a remembered chat plan.
