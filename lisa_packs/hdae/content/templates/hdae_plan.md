# HDAE Plan

Shape confirmed intent into proportionate, reviewable work. A small bounded
request may need a concise outcome, scope, evidence and stop point instead of
a durable gate. For iterative work, propose observable gates, dependencies,
positive and negative criteria, verification outcomes and coherent delivery.
Review relevant coverage without expanding the requested goal.

Explain the proposed batch and meaningful checkpoint boundaries to the human.
Separate objective reversible work from consequential or subjective decisions.
Use `hdae_define_gate` for gate definitions and `hdae_authorize` for exact human
authorization; a plan or draft does not authorize implementation. Use
`hdae_publish_artifact` for project strategy and `hdae_status` for live state.
Operations own structure and authority checks; adequacy of scope, criteria and
checkpoint placement remains a human decision informed by model proposals.

Consult Decide whether a gate is needed, Mandatory gate alignment, Delivery
strategy and commit structure, and Multi-gate batches and checkpoint policy.
Preserve useful project conventions and prior decisions. Material changes
return to human direction; existing approval does not authorize a new gate.
