# HDAE Evaluate

Evaluate delivered behavior against the human's intent and approved criteria.
Inspect actual observations, failures, regressions, usability, documentation,
delivery and limitations. Distinguish objective evidence from advisory model
assessment and subjective human judgment. Explain what the chosen checks can
and cannot establish; passing tests does not prove the specification sufficient.

Use `hdae_verify` for technical reconciliation, `hdae_status` for authoritative
progress, `hdae_accept` for authenticated checkpoint review, and `hdae_export`
for inspectable evidence. Use `hdae_define_gate` to propose corrective work.
Operations own passage and review boundaries. Report technical passage awaiting
review honestly, and never describe confirmed delegated closure as individual
human acceptance. Missing required delivery remains incomplete.

Apply Verify, Maintain and Retrospective across artifacts. Consult Verify and
maintain, Gate quality, Traceability and completeness for long projects, and
Completion language. At a boundary, present state, evidence, unmet obligations
and the exact human decision or next action. Resume from live project evidence.
